Wanderer's notes

How to walk the vale

WASD↑←↓→

Walk. Hold Shift to run.

Tap

Tap or click the ground to walk there.

E

Examine a cottage, the pond, or the old brazier.

WheelPinch

Zoom the view. Use the minimap buttons too.

The five ember wisps drift near the pond, the northern meadow, the orchard, the south lane and the western grass. Gather them, then return to the brazier at the heart of the village.
